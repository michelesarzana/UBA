The Methodology
Phase 1 — Historical data

Three data phases

Merkle 2018–2023: monthly UBA tracking on fixed and mobile separately, Merkle CAWI/CATI methodology. Doxa 2024–2025: methodological continuity with Merkle, same panel. Kantar 2026+: new institute with single awareness measure (not split fixed/mobile). Kantar data is harmonised with a brand-specific correction factor calculated over the overlap period.

Phase 2 — AGCOM Weights

Brand-specific weights

For each brand and year, the fixed/mobile weight is calculated based on the real AGCOM customer base: W_fixed = CB_fixed / (CB_fixed + CB_mobile). Weights vary significantly across brands (Fastweb predominantly fixed; Iliad mobile-only until 2022) and over time (Fastweb's mobile share grows from 2% in 2018 to 26% in 2025).

Phase 3 — Kantar Harmonisation

Series continuity

The switch Doxa → Kantar (Jan 2026) introduces a systematic discontinuity. To ensure continuity, a correction factor k = avg(Doxa Oct–Dec 2025) / avg(Kantar Jan–Mar 2026) is calculated and applied to all Kantar data. Factors vary by brand: Fastweb +21.8%, TIM +7.2%, Vodafone +13.8%.

▶ How the Doxa → Kantar correction works

The problem: Doxa measured awareness separately for the fixed and mobile markets. Kantar measures brand awareness at a total level (all Italians). The two methods are not directly comparable: Kantar tends to record lower values because it also includes people who are not familiar with the specific fixed or mobile market of the brand.


The solution: For each brand, we calculate the harmonisation factor k = avg_Doxa_last3months / avg_Kantar_first3months. This factor captures the "systematic bias" between the two institutes for that specific brand. By applying k to Kantar values, we bring them onto the Doxa scale, making the series comparable over time.


Why it is brand-specific: The factor k differs for each brand because the bias depends on the brand's own fixed/mobile mix. Fastweb (historically fixed) has the highest factor (1.22) because its awareness in the fixed market was much higher than in the total market. Iliad (mobile-first) has the lowest factor (1.12) because it was already measured mainly on mobile.


Limitation: the correction assumes the bias is constant over time. If Kantar changes its methodology internally, the factor may become inaccurate. Post-harmonisation values should always be read with this in mind.

Methodological notes

The harmonised UBA KPI is calculated as a weighted average of Unaided Brand Awareness indices measured separately on fixed broadband and mobile customers (Human SIM). Annual weights are brand-specific and derived from the official customer base shares published by AGCOM in the Communications Observatories (2018–2026). The weighting reflects the fact that historically fixed brands (Fastweb) have a different customer base from predominantly mobile brands (Iliad) or balanced ones (TIM, Vodafone, WindTre).
